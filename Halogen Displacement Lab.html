Displacement of halogens: virtual lab

A more reactive halogen can push a less reactive halogen out of its compound. Test each halogen with each potassium halide solution and watch the colour carefully.

After the experiment: questions
  1. In which test tubes did the colour change? What does a colour change tell you?
  2. Put chlorine, bromine and iodine in order of reactivity, most reactive first.
  3. Complete the word equation: chlorine + potassium bromide → ________ + ________
  4. Explain why there was no reaction when iodine solution was added to potassium bromide.
  5. Stretch: write a balanced symbol equation for chlorine reacting with potassium iodide.

In a real lab, chlorine water is used in a fume cupboard. Halogens are toxic and irritate the skin, eyes and lungs. Wear eye protection.
