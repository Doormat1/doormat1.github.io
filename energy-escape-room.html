🚨 LAB LOCKDOWN!

Professor Joule has locked your team inside the science lab. The only way out is through a digital padlock on the door. To find the 5-digit code you must solve the puzzles in five rooms using what you know about energy stores and energy pathways.

  • Each room gives you one digit. Write it in the box at the bottom of the room, then move on.
  • Nobody will tell you if you are right! You only find out when you try the padlock at the end, so check your work carefully as a team.
  • Keep a note of your digits on paper too, just in case.
  • Stuck? Each room has a 💡 Hint, but talk it through with your team first.
